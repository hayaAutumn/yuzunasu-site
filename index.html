Yuzu & Nasu

Yuzu & Nasu is a series of short, wordless animated comedies about two friends: cheerful little Yuzu and tired, gentle Nasu. No dialogue, so anyone in the world can enjoy them.

New original episodes are published on YouTube (channel Yuzu & Nasu, @YuzuAndNasu) and TikTok (utakata2222).
