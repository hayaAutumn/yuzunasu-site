Privacy Policy — Yuzu & Nasu poster

Last updated: 2026-10-07

"Yuzu & Nasu poster" is a personal tool used by one person (the owner of the Yuzu & Nasu channels) to upload the owner's own animated videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

What we access. With the owner's permission, the tool receives an access token for the owner's YouTube channel (upload permission only) and the owner's TikTok account (basic profile and video publishing). It does not read, collect or store any other person's data.

What we store. The access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local file that is not shared. No personal data of any other user is collected, sold, shared or disclosed.

Third parties. Videos and captions are sent only to YouTube (Google) and TikTok through their official APIs. Their own privacy policies apply to data held by them.

Revoking access. The owner can revoke access at any time in the Google Account security settings and in TikTok's app permissions; the tool then stops working.
